RagdollLab

A physics sandbox for VR and desktop. Spawn crates, bombs, carts and customisable ragdoll NPCs, then grab, throw, freeze, tie up and blow up everything across five maps, from a warehouse to the Moon at 1.62 m/s². Twelve weapons, from a pistol to a freeze ray, live in the Weapons tab of the menu, and the Workshop tab lets you draw your own in the air.

Desktop

  • WASD move · Shift run · Space jump
  • Left click use tool · Right click/E grab
  • Wheel push/pull held item · R rotate it
  • 1–0 tools · Q next spawn item
  • Tab menu · Z undo · T slow-mo · G zero-g
  • Holding a weapon: Left click fire · Right click throw · F drop

VR controllers

  • Trigger use tool · Grip grab & throw
  • Left stick walk · Right stick turn
  • Right stick up/down while holding: reel in/out
  • X menu: point + trigger, or poke the buttons
  • A next tool · B next item · Y undo
  • Weapons: trigger fires · squeeze & let go to drop
  • Punch things with your hands
